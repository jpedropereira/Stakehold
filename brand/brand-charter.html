I. The mark

The keep

Battlements that ascend like a bar chart. The mark reads as a small stronghold at any size, and as a growth chart to anyone who looks twice — the same shape carries both ideas at once.

Clear space and minimum size

Give the mark breathing room equal to the width of one merlon on every side. Below 24px, drop the flag and door detail — see the app icon crop.

512px — full detail
60px — home screen
16px — favicon, no flag
Do

Place the gold mark on stone, or the stone mark on parchment. Keep the two-tone contrast.

Don't

Recolor the mark in an accent hue, add a drop shadow, or set it on a busy photo background.

Do

Simplify at small sizes — drop the door notch and flag first, the battlements last.

Don't

Stretch, skew, or rotate the mark. It stands upright, like the thing it depicts.

IV. Voice

Steady, plain, unhurried

Stakehold talks like someone who's managed money through a few bad quarters and didn't panic. Confident without hype, precise without jargon.

01

Plain verbs

"Add a position," not "Initiate a holding action." Say what happens.

02

No panic, no hype

Losses are stated flatly. Gains aren't celebrated with exclamation points.

03

Numbers do the talking

Let the figure carry the weight. Don't editorialize a red number.

04

Light on the theme

One or two words per screen at most — "the keep," "the watch." Never a full bit.

A position, rendered
